## 6. Assumptions & Known Limitations

1. **All electrical parameters are assumed** (source: assumed) — not verified with KPTCL/SLDC data. See `config/topology.yaml`.
2. **Chain topology only** — no meshed network, no parallel paths other than the double-circuit representation.
3. **Shunt capacitors are always-on** — no tap-changer or switchable compensation. Ferranti effect causes vm > 1.05 pu at light loads.
4. **Theft is modelled as a constant-power load** — no time-of-day variation in the theft behaviour within a scenario.
5. **Non-converged PF cases are skipped** — these occur for extreme line outage combinations and are not represented in the dataset.
6. **Sensor bias/drift magnitudes are assumed** — ranges chosen to be physically plausible for utility-grade SCADA but not calibrated to real KPTCL meters.
7. **Small theft (1–3 MW) overlaps with noise** — intentional, to represent the practical sensitivity limit. Do not tune noise to artificially improve detection of small thefts.
